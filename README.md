## ⚡ About Me

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at **VIT Vellore** (CGPA 9.14/10, Class of 2027) working at the intersection of three connected threads: **VLSI/ASIC & RTL design**, **analog + PCB hardware** (especially biosignal front-ends), and **embedded systems / embedded AI**. Most of what I build lands at the overlap of **biomedical electronics and hardware ML** — EEG/ECG acquisition, arrhythmia detection, and health monitoring — with a side interest in hardware security, neuromorphic computing, and optical fiber communications.

Beyond the bench, I'm **Secretary of IEEE Circuits and Systems (CAS) at VIT**, and I've picked up industrial-side experience too — assembly-line maintenance and PLC-based automation at **Toyota Kirloskar Motor**, and industrial IoT / condition monitoring at **Schneider Electric**.

### 🧠 SNN AFib Detector — Spiking Reservoir ASIC
**Verilog HDL · TinyTapeout · SKY130A PDK** &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Taped out Apr 2026*

A 959-cell event-driven ASIC for real-time AFib and asystole detection. Five-stage Verilog pipeline — RR feature extraction → spike encoding → 8-neuron LIF reservoir with recurrent feedback → dual-window readout. Gate-level optimization (sign-extension, MSB comparators, bit-slicing) cut area and pushed power into the sub-µW range.

[`→ View repository`](https://github.com/AbishekvGuru/SNN_AFib_Detector)

### 🫀 Single-Channel EEG Acquisition Board
**KiCad · LTspice · Embedded C** &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Nov 2025*

Fabricated an EEG analog front-end (instrumentation amp + 0.5–50 Hz band-pass filter) with driven-right-leg circuitry. LTspice-validated: **14,500 V/V** measured gain vs. 15,000 V/V target, **98% suppression** of 50 Hz interference, low-noise layout throughout.

### 📡 Portable Multi-Sensor Health Monitor
**ESP32 · KiCad · Python** &nbsp;·&nbsp; *3 PCB revisions, Oct 2025*

ESP32-based PCB integrating ECG (AD8232), SpO₂/HR (MAX30100), and motion (MPU6050), streaming to a cloud dashboard. A 1D-CNN on 5,655 PhysioNet ECG recordings hits **93.8% accuracy / 75% recall** for AFib; a stacked LightGBM+LSTM ensemble hits **0.95 AUC** for sepsis-risk prediction.

</td>
