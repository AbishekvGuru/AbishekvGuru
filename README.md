## About

I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at VIT Vellore (CGPA 9.14/10, Class of 2027), working across VLSI/ASIC and RTL design, analog and PCB hardware, and embedded systems. My interest sits at the layer where a signal becomes a working circuit — from gate-level logic and IC tapeout flows to instrumentation-grade analog front-ends and firmware for real sensor systems. I'm also interested in hardware security, neuromorphic computing, and optical fiber communications.

Outside coursework, I serve as Secretary of IEEE Circuits and Systems (CAS) at VIT, organizing technical events and mentoring members in circuits, embedded systems, and PCB design. I've also worked in industrial settings — assembly-line maintenance and PLC automation at Toyota Kirloskar Motor, and industrial IoT / condition monitoring at Schneider Electric.
